    About VerseList

    What it's for

    Read a list of Bible verses together on one page, or build and arrange a list of your own, then copy it or share it as a link.

    How it works

    • Type or paste references into the box, one per line or comma-separated. "John 3:16", "1cor 13:4-7", "Romans 8" and "Ephesians" all work.
    • The box always holds your list. Add, change or delete lines there and press Update List, or drag ☰ and press × on the cards.
    • A line under the buttons tells you whether the box and the cards match.
    • As Entered shows your own order; Bible Order sorts Genesis to Revelation without losing yours.
    • The copy buttons and Copy Link use the order shown. Copy All Verses includes the verse text; Copy as List and Copy as Array give just the references.
    • Each card's heading opens that passage in Bible Peruser.

    Part of Gospel Go

    VerseList is one of the Gospel Go Bible apps, alongside Bible Peruser and Bible Explorer. The other apps can hand a list of verses to VerseList by link, and it works just as well on its own.

    Privacy

    No account and no tracking. Your list is kept in your browser and in the link you choose to share.
